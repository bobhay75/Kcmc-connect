# KCMC Connect: basic administrator guide

This guide describes the review candidate. The church should use it only after the candidate has been installed and tested on its own hosting.

## Sign in

Open KCMC Connect and choose **Administration**. Sign in using a church administrator account. If you cannot sign in, contact the person responsible for the church's hosting and administrator access. The public app does not require an account.

## Update church information

On **Church content**, review the contact information, featured announcement, Sunday bulletin and event fields. Enter only details confirmed by the church. Check dates and event visibility carefully. Select **Publish changes**, review the confirmation, and confirm. Visit the public page afterward to verify the result.

If the editor says content changed, do not immediately reload and lose your work. Copy your unsaved text, open the latest version as instructed, compare it and re-enter only the intended changes.

## Add or remove an event

Use **Add event** to enter its details and save. Use **Manage posts** to hide, move an event to Trash, or restore it for review. Check the public events page after making changes.

## Review responses

Choose **Connection requests** for visit, group and serving inquiries, or **Event responses** for RSVPs. Treat contact information as private. Use the available follow-up status controls to keep track of responses and download a CSV only when needed, storing it securely.

## Sign out

Use **Sign out** at the top of the Church content page when finished, especially on a shared computer.

## Backups and support

Content edits create backups in the app's configured backup directory. The current candidate does not include an administrator backup-download or restore screen. The hosting administrator must maintain and test secure backups of files, content, configuration and private records, and document recovery before handoff. Administrator account recovery and transfer must also be arranged before delivery.
