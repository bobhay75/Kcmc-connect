Join Pastor Tony for the Way Forward All Church Informational Meeting on Thursday, October 22 at 1:30 PM. Come hear the information and be part of the conversation.

Trunk or Treat volunteers: welcome families with us

Help us reach at least 30 decorated trunks! Sign up to bring your vehicle and welcome children and families. The pastors have promised a party for participants if we reach 30 trunks. Volunteers should arrive by 3:30 PM on October 31 to park and decorate. Plan for approximately 200 children: about 200 individually wrapped treats for one each, or 400 for two each. Use commercially packaged, individually wrapped treats. Extra candy is available, and the church can provide candy if purchasing it is a burden. Drop off donated candy at the church office or in the lobby. Keep decorations and optional costumes family-friendly and not frightening; an adult volunteer costume contest is planned. Keep walkways clear and cords secure. Keep vehicles parked until the event is over and families have cleared the area. Smile, welcome each child and be patient with shy visitors. Contact the church office with questions. Contact the church office about signing up a trunk. The public event runs October 31, 4:30–6:30 PM in the church parking lot, with a bounce house, hot dogs, hot cider and a costume contest.
